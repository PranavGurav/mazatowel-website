# MazaTowel Asset Notes

This build avoids paid stock-photo, paid icon-pack, and paid UI-template dependencies.

- UI icons are inline SVG authored for this project.
- Product/design/gallery photos should be uploaded through the Admin panel using images owned or licensed by MazaTowel.
- The MazaTowel logo included in `public/assets/` is supplied by MazaTowel for its website.
- Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts at runtime (Manrope and DM Serif Display) and are not bundled in this project.
- Do not upload third-party copyrighted images, logos, characters, or artwork unless you have permission to use them.
